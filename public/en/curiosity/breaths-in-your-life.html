A machine that never stops

We breathe around 16 times per minute on average, which equals nearly 23,000 breaths a day. We do it without thinking — the autonomic nervous system keeps the lungs going even while we sleep. It is arguably the most repeated action of our entire lives.

Breathing curiosities

  • Newborns breathe much faster: between 30 and 60 times per minute.
  • During intense exercise we can reach 40-60 breaths per minute.
  • The world static apnea record exceeds 24 minutes, set by Budimir Šobat in 2021.
  • Each breath moves about 0.5 litres of air. In a day, your lungs process more than 11,000 litres.

Frequently asked questions

How many times do we breathe per minute?

A healthy adult breathes between 12 and 20 times per minute at rest. The average is around 16.

How many times do we breathe per day?

Around 23,000 times a day and more than 8 million times a year at 16 breaths per minute.

Can we control our breathing?

Yes — it is one of the few automatic functions we can consciously control. That is why meditation and yoga use it as a stress-reduction tool.
